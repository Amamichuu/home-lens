# Vision

## Mission

Help people make better housing decisions with less effort.

## Problem

Searching for an apartment is often overwhelming.

People open dozens of browser tabs, compare listings manually, lose interesting properties, and end up relying on spreadsheets or memory.

## Solution

HomeLens provides a simple workspace where users can collect, compare and evaluate apartment listings in one place.

## Principles

- Simplicity first
- Save users time
- Reduce decision fatigue
- Beautiful and intuitive experience
